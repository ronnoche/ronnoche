Stress pt.1: "I don't know what I'm doing."

Jan 11, 2023
Advocacy

Burnout and work-related stress are increasingly common. Over the past year, I've had conversations with people feeling exhausted from work or studies. Their stories reflect a struggle many of us share.

Why are we always so tired? Based on my learning from the Science of Well-being course and personal observations, one common cause is uncertainty about what to do.

“I don't know what I'm doing.”

When we're unsure about the steps to take, we tend to overthink. This overthinking drains our mental energy and can lead to anxiety. Often, it triggers procrastination, followed by guilt and self-blame—trapping us in a cycle that leads to burnout.

Common Examples:

  • You have a long to-do list and don't know where to start → You freeze → You procrastinate.
  • You're assigned a task but don't know how to begin → You delay → You feel guilty → You freeze → You procrastinate.

The Cycle:

Overthinking → Procrastination → Self-blame → Burnout.

What Can We Do?

Take action. Start — even if it's messy. Confront the task. Progress, not perfection, is key. As cliché as it sounds, sometimes the best way out is through.

Stress from unfinished work often lingers until the task is done. So, start. Take small steps. Finish it, even if it's imperfect.
