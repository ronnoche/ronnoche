Stress pt2: "I don't like what I'm doing"

January 18, 2023
Advocacy

Another common cause of work-related stress is the feeling of being stuck in something we don't like. "I don't like what I'm doing."

This is often more complex because there are deeper reasons behind it. Maybe your parents pressured you into your college course. Maybe you took a job because you needed to provide for your family. Maybe circumstances led you to a path you would not have chosen for yourself.

Regardless of the reason, there's a common thread: It's hard to fully commit when it wasn't entirely your decision.

What Can We Do?

There's no one-size-fits-all solution, but one approach that helps many is this: Pursue a passion project.

A passion project is something you choose for yourself. It gives you a sense of control, ownership, and autonomy—reminding you that not everything is dictated by circumstances.

Examples:

  • If you're working a job to support your family, try starting a side project that excites you.
  • If you're a student in a course you don't like, consider joining an organization or starting a small creative project.

Passion projects can spill over into other areas of life. They ignite curiosity, creativity, and sometimes, lead to unexpected opportunities.

When we take action on what we care about, we slowly regain our sense of direction. It reminds us: "This is my choice."
