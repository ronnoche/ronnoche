Journal 19: "I'm not supposed to be anything."

March 12, 2023
Reflection

"What do you want to be when you grow up?" A question asked with innocence, yet one that lingers heavily as the years unfold.

"How was I supposed to know?"

"How am I supposed to know?"

I am not supposed to be anything. I am not a goal to be reached or a title to be claimed. I just want to be — myself.

But who is that? I do not know. I still do not know.

And perhaps that is okay.
