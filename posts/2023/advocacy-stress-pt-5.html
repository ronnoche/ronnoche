Stress pt5: "I can't afford to rest"

February 8, 2023
Advocacy

“I can't afford to rest.” This is something we often tell ourselves when the workload is overwhelming and deadlines are pressing. But here's the truth: You can. You must.

Rest is not the opposite of work; it is part of work. Without rest, work suffers. Without pause, productivity declines.

Rest Is More Than Sleep

We often confuse rest with sleep, but rest is broader. Rest means doing something that refuels you — something where you don't need to perform or worry about getting it right.

Examples of Rest:

  • Spending time with family.
  • Reading a book for pleasure.
  • Taking a walk or hiking.
  • Enjoying quiet time, meditation, or personal reflection.
  • Engaging in a hobby without pressure.

Why Rest Matters:

Rest restores your energy and creativity. It prevents burnout and allows you to show up fully when you return to work.

Ironically, rest helps you work better. When you pause, you recharge. When you breathe, you create space for clarity and focus.

Final Thought:

You do not need to earn rest. Rest it not something you do after work. Rest is a part of work.
Find what gives you peace — breathe — and keep going.
