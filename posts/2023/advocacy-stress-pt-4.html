Stress pt4: "I don't know why I'm working"

February 1, 2023
Advocacy

Work-related stress isn't always about workload or people—it can also stem from a deeper question: "Why am I even doing this?"

This is more than just frustration; it's an existential concern. As humans, we seek meaning in what we do, and when work starts feeling empty, it's difficult to stay motivated.

How Do We Deal With This?

While there isn't a single answer, one helpful mindset shift is this: "Not everything needs a purpose right now."

Many people struggle with motivation because they don't immediately see the significance of their work — whether it's filling spreadsheets, writing reports, or preparing presentations. But meaning is often revealed after the task is completed, not before.

Reframing Work:

  • Sometimes, our impact isn't visible to us, but that doesn't mean it isn't real.
  • We may not control the outcome of our work, but we can control the effort we put in.
  • Work itself — regardless of its outcome — can shape us into better, more skilled individuals.

Finding Meaning in Process and Product:

Meaning isn't always found in the final result. Sometimes, the act of working itself teaches patience, discipline, and resilience.

Even when we don't immediately see our impact, we continue. And in time, the purpose often reveals itself.

Yet if not, we can always say "I overcame. I've worked. I've grown."
