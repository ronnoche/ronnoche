Stress pt3: "I don't like who I work with"

January 25, 2023
Advocacy

Workplace stress often stems not just from tasks but from the people we work with.

"I don't like who I work with." — a is a reality many of us face.

While it's easy to label people as "toxic," it's often more helpful to view behaviors as toxic, not individuals. So, how do we deal with difficult work relationships?

Ask Ourselves These Three Questions:

1. Is this a non-significant relationship?

If the person is not your boss, supervisor, teammate, or someone directly affecting your work—ignore them. Let them be. Their opinions should not hold power over you.

2. Do I work with them directly?

If yes, and their behavior affects you, approach your work relationship professionally:

  • Set boundaries. Accept work-related input; filter the rest.
  • Maintain respect but protect your peace.
  • Find your “tribe” — people who uplift you. Build that support system.

3. What if I feel like everyone is toxic?

It may be time for deeper reflection:

  • Check your perspective. Are you contributing to the tension? Can you ever see yourself being wrong?
  • Consider changing your environment if it consistently drains you.

Final Thoughts:

There's no universal solution to work stress from people. But asking these questions can offer clarity and help you regain a sense of control.

Choose your battles. Protect your energy. And when possible, choose the people who help you grow.
