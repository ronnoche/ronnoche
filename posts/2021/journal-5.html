Journal 5: denial of reality

May 9, 2021
Journal

It is exhausting to hope. To look forward. To believe that tomorrow will be better while the world constantly shows me otherwise.

I cling to faith, but at times, even faith feels like an escape — a shield painted with positivity to avoid the sharpness of reality.

But faith was never meant to blind me. It was never meant to pull me away from the present. Faith is not the denial of reality; it is the strength to stand in it.

To believe while my feet remain grounded in the present. To see the world as it is and still choose to walk forward.

Faith is not always about waiting for what will be. Sometimes, it is finding the courage to live fully with what is.

Here. Now.
