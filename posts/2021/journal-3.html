Journal 3: works look different

March 18, 2021
Journal

Sometimes work is frustrating. It doesn't work out as I planned.

No results? I remind myself to start moving.
Bad results? I pause and check what's not working.
Great results? I celebrate — I worked for this.

I'm learning that what works look different for everyone. Some people work best after rest. Some after a detailed plan. Others figure it out by trial and error. And some need to talk it out with others before finding clarity.

I used to think consistency was the ultimate key to success. But I'm not a machine — I'm human. I get tired. I need breaks. My rhythm changes.

What matters more than consistency is understanding how I work best. Finding my process — my pace — my way.

That's the key.
