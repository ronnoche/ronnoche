Journal 8: what if it were simple?

December 29, 2021
Journal

Why everything is complicated?

Most of my burdens — my exhaustion, my resistance — can be traced back to complicated systems I have unknowngly built around me.

A morning routine cluttered with perfection.
A workday tangled with overplanning.
A life strained by processes that demand too much.

But what if it were simple?

If it is simple, it is doable.
If it is doable, it is repeatable.
If it is repeatable, it becomes effortless.

A simple system liberates the mind. It lightens the load. It allows me to focus on what matters.

When my habits feel heavy, I remind myself — pause. Ruminate.
How can I simplify this?

To simplify is to systematize.

I want need to create systems that always simplifies.

~ Ron
