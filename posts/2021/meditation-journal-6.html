Meditation Journal 6

July 3, 2021
Journal

I have to remind myself:
"I shouldn't rush in a year what I will do for the rest of my life."

What I wish to do for the rest of my life need not be perfected in a single streak. My journey is not a race.

If I missed reading for an hour today, it is fine.
If I skipped one set of exercise this week, it is fine.
If I forgot to meditate and journal this morning, it is fine.
If I did not do deep learning for two hours today, it is fine.

Missing a day is not failure — it is life unfolding.

My habits are not slow to develop. I am simply in a hurry.

But why hurry?

If this is what I will do for the rest of my life, let me pace myself. Let me enjoy the process, knowing that progress is not lost in a day but built over a lifetime.

"I am not slow. I am just in a hurry."

~ Ron
