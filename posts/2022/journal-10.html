Journal 10: easier to be me

March 27, 2022
Journal

I sometimes struggle to live in a world I constructed for myself. A world filled with expectations and a version of me that is not quite me.

"I should be this." "I have to do that." These voices whisper, shaping the facade I wear.

I play the role of a god in my mind — grasping for control, demanding that life tilts in my favor. I grow restless when it does not.

But the world does not revolve around me. It never did. It never will.

This truth humbles me. It frees me. It allows me to let go of the exhausting repetitive performances.

"It's not about me" is not defeat. It is liberation. It is the invitation to simply be me.

It is easier to be me, in a world that doesn't revolve around me.
