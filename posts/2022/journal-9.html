Journal 9: meaninglessness

May 25, 2022
Journal

The Universe is not made with me in mind. It does not owe me an explanation. It has no obligation to make sense, especially to me.

The mere fact that I live my life with hesitations because the universe offers me neither an explanation nor meaning is very egotistical to say the least. As if my living is contingent to meaning.

Arrogance urges me to negotiate with the Universe that I have meaning and everything I do has a greater purpose. And if satisfied, I can live my life to the fullest.

But why negotiate?

Why not accept the meaninglessness the Universe offers and live life to the fullest? That meaning is not paramount to living.

This humbles me.

~ Ron
