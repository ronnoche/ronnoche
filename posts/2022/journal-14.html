Journal 14: What truly matters right now?

August 19, 2022
Philosophy

There are days when life feels heavy. The to-do list is long, and the pressure to keep up can be overwhelming.

When I catch myself feeling this way, I pause and ask —
What truly matters right now?

Not everything needs my attention immediately.
Not everything deserves my energy.

Focusing on what is essential helps clear the noise. It reminds me that I don't have to do it all at once.

Letting go of the nonessential brings clarity. It gives me space to breathe.

So, when the day feels overwhelming — pause.
Focus on what matters.
Let the rest wait.

That's often enough.

~ Ron
