Journal 18: "Meaning behind my pain"

November 21, 2022
Reflection

I used to search for meaning in my suffering. That “one day,” everything will just make sense. That my pain would be justified.

But sometimes, it doesn't. Most of the time, it will not. And perhaps, it doesn't have to.

Pain, often, carries no grand reason. It simply is.

I once asked, “Did I suffer meaninglessly?” Now, I answer, “I experienced pain.”

Not everything will have meaning — especially suffering. And the desperate search for it often prolongs the ache.

So, I stopped looking. And I started living.
